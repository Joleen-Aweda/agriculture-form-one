Market research:

When selling table eggs, it is important to understand what customers want. This can be done through market research, which involves asking questions or observing people to learn about their preferences and trends. For example, you can ask people how much they are willing to pay for eggs, what kind of packaging they like, and who they think the target market for eggs is. By understanding what customers like, you can make your marketing strategies better.

Marketing strategies:

Marketing strategies help business people to promote table eggs effectively. Here are some simple explanations of key marketing strategies you can use:
(a) Use of unique selling proposition: This means finding something special about your eggs that makes them different from others. It could be the way they are produced or their special qualities. For example, if your eggs come from free-range chickens or organic eggs, you can highlight that as your unique selling proposition.
(b) Creating a strong brand identity for your eggs: Creating a strong brand identity means giving your eggs a recognisable and memorable image. This can include a logo, packaging design, and a catchy name. When people see your brand, they should instantly know it is your eggs.
(c) Use digital marketing: This involves using digital platforms such as social media to promote your eggs. You can create a social media marketing platform(s) where people can learn about your eggs and place orders. Social media platforms such as Instagram, WhatsApp and Facebook can help you reach a wider audience.
(d) Participating in local markets and events: Participating in local markets and events allows you to showcase and sell your eggs directly to customers. It is a great way to connect with the community and build relationships with customers.
