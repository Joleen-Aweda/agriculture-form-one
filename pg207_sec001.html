2. Manage the broiler chicken from day-old to selling point.
(a) Manage the brooder for your broiler chickens: Prepare a brooder for the chicks. Provide the right temperature, ventilation and humidity as well as 23 hours of light and 1 hour of darkness. Note the effects of these conditions on the chicks.
(b) Manage the feeding of your broiler chickens: Feed the chicks using the three feed types feeding schedule or the two feed types feeding schedule. Follow all the guidelines of feeding you have learnt. Note the growth and development of the chickens.
