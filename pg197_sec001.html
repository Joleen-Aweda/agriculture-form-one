Activity 11.3

Plan and manage the layer chickens from day-old chick to final culling. Prepare recording tools and keep all the important physical and financial records as discussed in the chapter. Record your observations and experiences during the activity including the steps you took, the challenges you faced, and the lessons you have learnt. Consider the following phases in doing this activity.

Phase 1

Select a suitable breed and order DOC. You can enquire and list down different types of layer chicken breeds available in layer chick sellers. Find out the advantages and challenges of each breed. Based on your findings, select the best layer chicken breed for your area by considering the market demand and your personal preference. Thereafter, prepare to obtain resources required for rearing layer chickens from day-old chicks to the 8th week in your school and if possible, at home. Prioritize to make a maximum use of locally available resources then perform the tasks that follow:

  1. 1. Prepare the brooding house, brooder, heat sources, feeders, drinkers, litter, and feeds, and arrange them properly ready for receiving day-old chicks. If there is no ready-made house, you can construct one by using locally available resources.
  2. 2. Manage handling of chicks at arrival and keep all the required records.
  3. 3. Manage temperatures in the brooding house using the thermometer or watching chicks’ distribution.
  4. 4. Manage ventilation in the brooding house by practising opening and closing the curtains.
  5. 5. Manage lighting schedule in the brooding house as the chicks grow.
  6. 6. Manage humidity levels in the brooding house by using a hygrometer.
  7. 7. Manage feeding by weighing the right amount of feed using the scale and feeding chicks based on their age.
  8. 8. Manage stocking rate by adjusting the size of the brooder in the brooding house by considering the stocking density.
  9. 9. Carry out disease and parasite preventive measures by administering vaccines and prophylactics at the right time using the right method.
