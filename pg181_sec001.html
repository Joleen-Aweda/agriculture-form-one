Management of stocking rate for layer chicks

During the brooding period, a stocking density of 50 chicks per square metre is recommended. When housing density is increased, feeding space must also be increased. This is because as the chicks grow, the floor space allowance becomes small. It is recommended to start with a floor space allowance of 64 cm² per chick during the first week and increase this allowance by 64 cm² per chick weekly until the birds are 5 - 6 weeks old.

Management of parasites and diseases for layer chicks

Follow all measures you have learnt in the previous chapter to prevent chicks from parasites and diseases. Where diseases arise, treat the chicks immediately with the advice of a veterinarian. Chicks must be vaccinated correctly and on schedule against Newcastle, Infectious Bursal disease (Gumboro) and Fowl pox. The first vaccination against Marek’s disease is done at the hatchery and the rest on the farm, if necessary. A sample of the vaccination schedule is shown in Table 11.3.
