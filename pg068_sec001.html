The use of inorganic fertilisers in maintaining soil fertility

Inorganic fertilisers are made in factories and may contain Nitrogen, Phosphorus and Potassium (NPK) and other nutrient elements. There are two types of fertilisers: single and mixed or compound fertilisers. Single fertilisers have only one nutrient. Mixed or compound fertilisers have two or more of the primary mineral nutrients. When a fertiliser contains all the three primary mineral nutrients, it is called a complete fertiliser. Inorganic fertilisers have different properties. These properties tell us how and when to use them and what rules we need to follow when using them.
