• (b)From day 19 to 35, feed them broiler grower pellets.
  • (c)From day 36 until they are sold, feed them broiler finisher pellets.

The feeding schedule for dual-purpose hybrid chickens raised for eggs is as follows:

  • (a)For the first 2 weeks, feed them with broiler starter crumble.
  • (b)From 2 to 10 weeks, switch to layer starter mash.
  • (c)
    From 10 to 4 months, feed them with layer grower mash. This is a period when the chickens start laying.
  • (d)At 5 months, feed them with a complete layer mash until final culling.

Note:These feeding schedules are guidelines.It is important to monitor the bird’s health and adjust the feeding schedule as needed.Sometimes, commercial dual-purpose chicken feed is available and cheaper.This can reduce your cost of production.
