Harvesting and postharvesting management of tomato

Tomato takes an average of 70 - 90 days to mature and become ready for harvesting depending on variety and climatic conditions. Maturity indices of tomatoes include an increase in fruit size, a change in skin colour and an increase in juice and sugar content. The main criterion is fruit colour change.
