Handling, value addition and marketing of indigenous chicken products

Eggs from indigenous chickens are collected and kept on clean trays. If trays are not available, they can be collected in baskets or plastic containers which let air pass through. If required, the eggs are graded based on external quality factors such as shape, texture and size. Once graded, eggs should be stored properly to maintain their quality. Eggs should be properly packed and distributed to customers. For meat, chickens should be sold alive or slaughtered at the appropriate age depending on the needs of the market. The meat is then processed and packaged for distribution and sale. Since indigenous chickens have no uniform characteristics as exotic broilers, it is better to sell them alive or as whole chickens. All measures to meet customers’ needs, quality of the product and safety should be properly observed.

Record keeping in indigenous chickens

Keeping records is very important in indigenous chicken production. Just like with other chickens, it is necessary to keep proper records. Make sure to enter records frequently. This will help you to manage your chicken farm effectively and efficiently.
