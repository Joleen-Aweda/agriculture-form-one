Ecological requirements and site selection for tomato production

Tomatoes grow in the soil that is well-drained, such as sandy loamy or clay loamy rich in organic matter. The soil should be slightly acidic with a pH between 5.5 and 6.5. Tomatoes like warm weather. They grow well between 20 - 25°C in the day and 15 - 17°C at night. Temperatures above 35°C or below 10°C can harm the plant. Tomatoes need more than 600 mm of rainfall in a growing season. Too much rain can cause leaf diseases. Also, irregular watering can damage the tomato fruits. Tomato needs at least 6 hours of direct sunlight during flowering. Too much sunlight can cause damage to the fruits. To choose a good place to grow tomatoes, you should look for a place that gets at least 6 hours of direct sunlight each day.
