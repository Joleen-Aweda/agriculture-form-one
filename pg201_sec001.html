Management of health for broiler chickens

To maintain a good health of the chicken and attain optimal growth and weight, some preventive and curative measures against parasites, diseases and vices are necessary. Follow the recommended vaccination schedule presented in Table 12.2.

Observe and note any signs of disease and provide treatment at any time as deemed necessary and/or as advised by your veterinarian. Deworming may be done if the birds are to be kept longer than 5 - 8 weeks. Avoid visitors to stop bringing/spreading bacterial and other disease-causing agents from outside into your flock. While working at the farm, you are expected to have and use farm working gears such as gumboots, overalls, and dust capes to ensure maximum biosecurity.
