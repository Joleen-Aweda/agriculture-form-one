Plant population (plant density)

the number of plants per unit area

Poultry

a general term for domesticated birds

Productivity

the efficiency of agricultural production measured by the quantity of crops or livestock produced per unit of land, labour, or capital that reflects resource optimisation and output

Prophylactics

substances or measures designed to prevent disease or infection, including drugs, vaccines, and other preventive interventions

Pullets

female growing fowls at 9 weeks to 20 weeks

Purebred

offspring resulted from true breeding

Rations

the specific amount and type of feed provided to an animal, usually on daily basis, to meet its nutritional needs

Screen house

a protective structure in agriculture, made of mesh or netting, designed to shield crops from pests, control climate, and facilitate plant growth while ensuring adequate ventilation and sunlight

Seedbed

a prepared area of soil where seeds are sown or planted, providing suitable conditions for germination and growth of plants

Seedling

a young plant that has recently sprouted from a seed. Seedlings are in the early stages of growth, with underdeveloped stems, leaves, and root systems, requiring care and nurturing

Slaughter

to kill animals for meat

Soil texture

a relative proportion of sand, silt, and clay particles in the soil, which influences its drainage, aeration, and fertility

Sowing

the act of planting seeds in the ground or other growing medium, initiating the process of germination and plant growth

Sustainable agriculture

farming that meets the needs of existing and future generation
