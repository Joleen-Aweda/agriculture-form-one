Marketing of live broiler and broiler meat

Broilers from your farm can be sold alive if it is the form preferred by the customers.

The broiler can be processed and sold as whole carcasses or cuts depending on the customer’s preference.

Marketing live broiler chickens and broiler meat involves complying with marketing standards, obtaining necessary certifications, understanding farming method marks, implementing effective marketing strategies, considering seasonal demand, and adhering to ethical practices.

Record keeping for broiler enterprise

Like in the layers production unit, record keeping is important to evaluate your enterprise.

Records on mortality, feed consumption, disease prophylaxis and treatment as well as sales and purchases must be kept daily.
