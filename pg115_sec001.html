Nursery preparation for tomato seedling production

You can grow tomato seedlings in two ways: on trays or in the soil.

Procedures for growing on a tray

  • (i)Choose trays with cells, for example, 105, 128 or 200;
  • (ii)Fill the trays with soil such as compost or peat moss;
  • (iii)Put one seed in the middle of each cell, half a centimetre deep;
  • (iv)Cover the seed with soil, but not too much;
  • (v)Keep the trays safe, away from the sun and rain; then,
  • (vi)Water them with a sprayer to keep the soil moist.

After 20 to 30 days, your seedlings are ready to move.

Procedures for growing in soil

  • (i)Make a seed bed 50 to 100 cm wide, 15 to 20 cm length;
  • (ii)Add a well-decomposed manure to the soil;
  • (iii)Clean the soil with dry grasses and straws, or chemicals;
  • (iv)Water the soil, but not too much;
  • (v)Make rows in the soil 15 to 20 cm apart;
  • (vi)Put seeds in the rows, and cover them with soil;
  • (vii)Cover the seeds with dry grass mulch, and lightly water them;
  • (viii)Remove the grass after 5 to 7 days when the seeds have germinated;
  • (ix)Protect the seedlings by putting an overhead shade; then,
  • (x)Water them as needed, remove weeds, and control pests.

After 20 to 30 days, you can move the seedlings to the main field.

Transplanting

Seedlings are ready to be moved to the main field at about 20 to 30 days from sowing (around 12 - 20 cm tall). Before transplanting, seedlings need to be hardened for a week by less watering. This helps them to be strong when they are moved.

The following are some important practices to follow when transplanting the seedlings:

  • (i)

    Make holes about 10 cm deep and diameter. Then, transfer each seedling with its soil from the nursery into these holes and cover them firmly with soil;

  • (ii)If you are transplanting seedlings in raised or flatbeds, transplant them 60 - 90 cm apart in the row (between the rows) and 50 - 60 cm apart in the same row (between the plants);
  • (iii)If you are growing seedlings using drip irrigation, transplant them at 120 - 150 cm apart in the row (between the rows) and 30 - 50 cm apart in the same row (between the plants);
