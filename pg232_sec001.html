Glossary

Agriculture

the practice of cultivating plants, raising animals, and producing food and other products used to sustain human life

Agritourism

a form of business enterprise that associates agricultural production and/ or processing with tourism by attracting visitors to a farm or agricultural business to entertain or educate them while generating income for the business owner

Azolla

an aquatic fern of family Salviniaceae consisting of short branched floating stem bearing roots which hang down in the water. It commonly lives in ponds, lakes swamps and streams in both tropic and subtropics conditions

Biological control

a method of controlling pests, diseases, or weeds using other living organisms

Biosecurity

hygiene and sanitation measures that are to be taken to prevent the spread of parasites and diseases to livestock

Breed

a group of animals that have similar appearance and are related to each other through a common ancestry

Broiler

chickens developed/raised for meat production

Brooder

an enclosure/protected area usually heated which is used for rearing Chicks/ducklings

Brooding

an art of rearing newly hatched chicks. It also means the period immediately after hatch when special care and attention must be given to chicks to ensure their health and survival

Cannibalism

an act of one individual of a poultry species consuming all or parts of another individual of the same species

Chick

a young chicken of either sex

Cock

an adult male chicken used for breeding

Crumble feed

a form of feed that has been pelleted and then broken down into smaller sizes

Culling

the act of removing unproductive birds in the flock
