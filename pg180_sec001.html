It is better to place some feed before the chicks on plate feeders (refer to Figure 10.5 (a)) or sheets of paper until the chicks learn to eat out of hopper feeders (refer to Figure 10.5 (b)). It is advisable to arrange feeders and drinkers in separate rows. To enable chicks reach feed and water points easily, rows for feeders should be alternated with those of drinkers (refer to Figure 9.9). Ensure ample room for comfort, eating and drinking. Provide well-placed feeders that will allow 7 - 12 cm per chick for up to 8 weeks. Use appropriate feeders for chicks at different stages of chick growth.

Drinkers should preferably be placed on raised wire platforms or boards. A water space allowance of 1.5 cm per chick for trough-type drinkers, one round automatic drinker for 70 -100 chicks or one nipple drinker per 20 chicks should be provided. Drinkers should be adjusted to the shoulder height of the birds to prevent litter from scratching into the water. Drinkers should be thoroughly cleaned and the water refilled frequently. It is advisable to disinfect the drinkers weekly. Table 11.2 shows the maximum and minimum feed allowance and weight in relation to the age of the chicks. This can be used to make follow-up whether the chickens are doing well or not.
