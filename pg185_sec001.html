Parasite and disease management in layer growers

The hygienic measures you have learnt in previous sections should be observed throughout the growing of the birds. Continue with the vaccination programme required in the growing stage. These include the Newcastle vaccine given every 75 – 90 days and the Fowl pox vaccine administered on the 10th week of age. The use of prophylactics to control parasites and diseases should be continued during the growing period with the advice of a qualified veterinarian. Likewise, regular deworming every 60 - 90 days by using proper anthelmintics should be continued with the advice of a qualified veterinarian. Proper litter management should be observed, for example, removing wet spots around the drinkers and replacing them with clean dry litter to reduce the build-up of coccidiosis disease-causing agents. This has to be done together with efforts to reduce water spillage from drinkers.

Beak trimming for layer growers

This is an act of reducing the beak of a bird by a properly trained person. It is also termed as debeaking. It is done to avoid egg eating, cannibalism, and other vices. It is also done to avoid spilling the feeds from the feeders. It can be done using an electric debeaker or manual debeaker (refer to Figures 11.1 (a) and (b)). Usually, less than a third of the upper beak is trimmed.
