(c) Storage: Store tomatoes at 12 - 15°C for best results. You can store them in a charcoal cooler or zero-energy cooling chamber where temperatures are a bit higher than this. You can store your tomatoes for 2 – 14 days.

(d) Marketing: You can sell tomatoes at the farm, local market, or even at distant markets. Common sizes are 20-litre plastic buckets and crates. At lower levels, 10-litre, 5-litre buckets, and bunches are popular. Prices can change quickly, often within a week. Generally, August to October are months with low tomato prices, while December to May are months with high prices in Tanzania.
