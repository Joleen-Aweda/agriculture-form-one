(i) Broadcasting: This method involves scattering seeds thinly and evenly over the area. When broadcasting vegetable seeds, you might mix them with a similar amount of sand (1 to 1 ratio) to keep them from sticking together and to make for a more even spread. This method is best for crops such as amaranth and lettuce, which have small and light seeds. These plants are shallow-rooted and can be easily thinned out later if needed.

(ii) Drilling: Drilling involves opening a shallow furrow in the soil and dropping seeds thinly in a furrow. This method is best for vegetables like carrots where the seeds are small and the plants need to be closely spaced. With drilling, there is a known space between rows but no clear spacing between plants within a row. It is also commonly practised when sowing onion or tomato seeds at the nursery seed-bed.

(iii) Direct row planting: This method is best for crops that need to be spaced out, for example, green beans, common peas and okra. These crops are large and they need to be spaced out for optimal growth. There is a known space between rows and within plants in rows.

(b) Transplanting: This method involves starting seeds in a controlled area (nursery), allowing them to grow and get stronger, and then transplanting the seedlings to the garden at the right time. Some vegetables that are best planted by transplanting include tomatoes, peppers, and onions.

Timely planting

Planting vegetables on time is key to growing them well and selling them when prices are high. Timely planting helps farmers to get the best yield from their land. If vegetables are planted too early or too late, they might not grow well, and farmers might not get enough yield. To sell vegetables when prices are high, they need to be planted at the right time. This can be tricky because it depends on the weather and soil type. Experienced farmers, teachers or agricultural extension workers can also advise on the best planting times. The goal is to plant vegetables when they will grow better and be ready for harvest when prices are high. This will help farmers make more money from their crops.
