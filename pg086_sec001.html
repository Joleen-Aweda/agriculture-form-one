Table 5.1: Easy guide to manage insect pests in amaranth

Pest

What they do

How to know them

How to manage them
Aphids Suck plant sap Curling leaves, stunted plants Use bio-pesticides, use aphid enemies
Stem weevils Mine plant stem Galls on stems Destroy affected parts
Leaf webbers Larvae chew leaves between veins White silky leaves, brown sawdust-like deposits Use resistant varieties, use bio-pesticides
Spider mites Suck plant sap Yellow or white spots on leaves, ball-like masses Use predators
