We can manage crop pests in different ways. We can use cultural farming practices, physical or mechanical methods, biological, chemical, or a combination of methods known as Integrated Pest Management (IPM).

Cultural pest management

Cultural pest management is a way to protect plants from pests using good farming practices. This is done by modifying the environment on which the pest lives. For example, we can modify the amount of sunlight, temperature, or the soil’s pH level. This involves the use of resistant varieties, timely planting and weeding, sanitation within and around the field use of mulching materials, removal of diseased plants and crop rotation. This method helps to prevent pests from becoming too many and damaging our crop plants. It makes our farming system stronger and it is less likely to need chemical pesticides.

Physical/mechanical pest management

These involve using physical or mechanical means to kill pests and create barriers to prevent pests from getting into contact with their target crops. The methods involved include hand picking, trapping and killing, flooding and overhead irrigation, physical barrier, proper drying before storage and use of lethal temperature.
