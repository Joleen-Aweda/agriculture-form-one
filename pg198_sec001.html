Phase 3

Prepare to obtain resources required for rearing mature layer chickens from the point of laying to final culling in your school and if possible, home environment.

Prioritize to make maximum use of locally available resources, then perform tasks that follow:

1.Manage the laying house as you have learnt.
2.Manage feeding including switching from grower to layer’s feed, weighing the right amount of feed using the scale and cleaning the feeders and drinkers.
3.Carry out parasite and disease preventive measures by administering vaccines and prophylactics at the right time and using the right method as well as curative measures if the need arises.
4.Manage egg collection as you have learnt.
5.Manage handling and storage as you have learnt.
6.Manage the selling of eggs as you have learnt.
7.Manage continuous and final culling as you have learnt.
