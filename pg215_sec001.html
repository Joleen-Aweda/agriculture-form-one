Exercise 13.1

1.

You have been tasked to design a house for your family’s indigenous chicken.

By considering location, size, and materials used.

How would you design this house in a way that ensures the chickens’ safety and comfort?

2.

Your farm is rich in resources such as maize, finger millet, sorghum, bulrush millet, cereal brans, pigeon peas, cowpeas and oil seed cakes.

How would you use these resources to create a comprehensive feeding programme for your indigenous chickens?

3.

Some of your indigenous chickens are falling sick frequently, despite your best efforts to maintain their health.

What steps would you take to identify the problem and resolve the health issues affecting your chickens?

4.

You have noticed that your indigenous chickens are not as lively as before.

They have pale red combs, and they do not lay as many eggs as expected.

You also see some small white spots moving quickly around the cloaca.

Suggest the possible causes of these symptoms and measures to take to ensure that your chicken are protected from these conditions.

5.

Imagine you are a farmer who wants to improve the meat and egg quality of your indigenous chickens.

How would you plan the mating process between hens and cocks to achieve this goal?

(Provide at least two strategies).

6.

If you have 20 hens, how many cocks should you have to ensure that most of the eggs are fertilised and are able to hatch into chicks?

Why is it important to carefully select the cocks for mating?

7.

Describe the steps you would follow to ensure successful incubation of indigenous chicken eggs using natural broody hens (natural hatching).

Include the egg selection criteria and the recommended time for candling the eggs during incubation.

8.

Imagine you are running an indigenous chicken enterprise that focuses on egg production.

Suggest three practical measures you would implement to ensure the quality and safety of the eggs during handling and value addition.

9.

You are a farmer who wants to keep accurate records of your indigenous chicken production:

(a) Explain three reasons why record-keeping is important in managing a chicken farm effectively.

(b) Provide examples of specific records you would maintain.
