Curing:

This is when we dry the parts of the onions that are above the soil. This helps to keep the onions fresh for longer. We can do this in the field or a special place if the weather is bad.

Drying:

The moisture on the onions should be reduced from about 86% to about 12%. In reducing it, use a process called ‘casing’. Cover the onions with straw or leaves to create a pile. Turn the pile regularly to let air circulate. This takes a few days to a few weeks, depending on the weather and the size of the crop. As the moisture decreases, the onions become harder and their smell changes. They also start to brown at the top, a sign that the moisture has been reduced.

Sorting and grading:

After the onions have been cured and dried, we sort and grade them. This is done to separate the onions based on their size, shape, and quality. This is to ensure easy packaging ready for marketing.

Storing:

Keep the onions in a cool place with a temperature of 25 - 35ºC. The air should be dry with a humidity of 65 - 70%. Onions are usually put in mesh bags. They can also be put in smaller bags for consumers. The onions need to be kept in a cool, dry place until they reach the consumers.

Marketing:

Once the onions are ready, they need to be sold or stored until the price in the market is high. You should use several ways to sell your onions for a good price and make a good income. These are some of the ways you can use to market your onions:

  • (a) Selling at the farm: You can sell your onions directly at your farm. This could be to local people who want fresh onions or to restaurants and hotels that need onions for their dishes.
  • (b) Storing and selling later: If the price of onions is low in the market, you can store your onions and sell them when the price is higher.
  • (c) Using digital marketing: You can use social media platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter to sell your onions. You can post pictures of your onions and their price. You can also share stories about how you grow your onions.
  • (d) Collaborating with food influencers: Food influencers are people who have many followers on social media. They can help you by sharing your opinions with their followers. For example, they can post pictures of your onions and recommend them to their followers.
