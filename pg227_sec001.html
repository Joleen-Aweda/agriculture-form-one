Handling, value addition and marketing of duck products

Duck products, for example, meat and eggs, are important for the farmers as well as the country’s economy. People buy these duck products to cook delicious meals. Selling ducks or duck products helps farmers to earn money. For farmers to earn profitable income, duck eggs and meat should be properly handled and marketed at a reasonable price.
