Planting of vegetable crops

This section will guide you through the essential practices in growing your vegetables.

Germination test

Germination is when a seed grows into a plant. This is a key step in planting. Before we plant our crops, we need to check if the seeds can germinate. We do this with a germination test. This test helps us see if the seeds are live and ready to grow. To do a germination test, follow this procedure:

  • (i)Pick seeds that look healthy.
  • (ii)Count 100 seeds.
  • (iii)Get some sand and put it in a container such as a bucket or a plate.
  • (iv)Put the seeds in the sand. If the container is small, you might need more than one container.
  • (v)Add water to the sand to moisten the seeds.
  • (vi)Keep watering the seeds to keep them moist but not wet until they germinate. How much you water, depends on how moist the sand is.
  • (vii)When the seeds grow, count them. The number of seeds that germinate will show how many out of 100 seeds you have sown. For example, if 96 out of 100 seeds grew, that means 96% of the seeds germinated.
