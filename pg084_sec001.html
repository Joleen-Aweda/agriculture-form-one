Preparation of seedbed for amaranth

To prepare a seedbed for amaranth, follow these steps:

(a)

Clear the site, then plough it to a depth of at least 25 centimetres. Afterwards, harrow and level the soil to create the finest tilth.

(b)

Remove weeds and debris to reduce competition for resources.

(c)

Apply a well-decomposed farmyard manure of about 25 tons per 1 hectare (about 2 kg per 1 m²) at the time of fine ploughing.

(d)

Use sunken beds during dry seasons when there is an insufficient amount of water; or use raised beds during excessive wet or rainy seasons.

(e)

Level the seed beds to ensure no depression patches on the seed bed surface to avoid damping off diseases.

Seed selection and planting of amaranth

Amaranth seeds come in many types, each with its own characteristic features. Some are good for grain, while others are better for leafy vegetables. It is important to buy high-quality seeds from trusted sellers, for example, research centres or registered seed distributors. If you can’t find registered seed sellers, you can buy seeds from local markets or grow your seeds at home. Before using them, it is important to check their germination rate. Amaranth seeds are very small, so during sowing, they are usually mixed with sand for easier handling. The common ratio is 1 gram of seed to 100 grams of sand. They are usually sown directly into the soil. Sometimes, they are grown in a nursery first, then later transplanted in the main field. However, this method is not practical for large fields.

Seeds can be directly sown in the field by broadcasting or sown in rows by drilling method. By broadcasting method, seeds mixed with sand are spread thinly and evenly over the seed bed. Sowing by drilling method requires you to go through the following procedures:

(a)

Make furrows that are 0.5 to 1.0 cm deep. Drill seeds mixed with sand into these furrows, then, lightly cover the seeds with soil.

(b)

When growing amaranth to be harvested by uprooting, keep furrows 15 - 20 cm apart. For amaranth picked by piece meal (bit by bit) space furrows 30 cm apart. After planting, thin out the plants to maintain a distance of 15 cm between each plant.

(c)

Water the bed gently to keep the field moist. The amount of water in the soil will decide how much and how often to irrigate.
