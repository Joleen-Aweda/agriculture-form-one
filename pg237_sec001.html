Thinning

the deliberate removal of excess plants or fruits from a crop to enhance the growth and quality of the remaining produce

Topdressing

the application of fertiliser, compost, or other soil amendments directly onto the soil surface before seedling or transplanting to enhance plant growth

Transplanting

the process of transferring seedlings from seedbeds/nurseries to the main field for continued growth

Ventilation

the circulation of fresh air through an enclosed space

Weed

a plant growing where it is not wanted, often competing with cultivated plants for resources and therefore, requiring removal or control
