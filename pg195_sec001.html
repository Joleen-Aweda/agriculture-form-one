Record keeping in the management of layer chickens

Record keeping is a process by which information is systematically collected, organised and stored.

Layer chicken production can be a highly competitive business which involves high capital for investment and high risks as well.

For the success of the enterprise, as the owner, you have to record all the farming activities from the beginning to the end of the laying cycle and do some analysis.

This will enable you to determine how your enterprise is performing, for example, to identify problems and solve them before it is too late.

It also helps you to control your cash and plan for the future.

Records for aspects such as mortality, egg production, feed consumption, growth rate, treatments or medication, expenditures and revenues must be kept daily.
