Activity 4.9

1.

Visit any nearby agricultural-related institution such as MATIs, and VTCs or any produce processor or events like ‘Nanenane’ or ‘Sabasaba’ exhibition to learn about the following:

(a) The different technologies used in vegetable crop postharvest practices.
(b) The tools, equipment, and machines used in vegetable harvesting and postharvesting practices.
2.

Use the ICT facilities to watch videos about:

(a) The different technologies used in vegetable harvesting and postharvesting.
(b) The scale of vegetable crop production where these technologies are used.
3. Write a summary of your observations in your portfolio.
4. Construct a charcoal evaporative cooler or zero-energy cooling chamber for storing vegetables from your school and home vegetable enterprises.

Exercise 4.7

1. If you were to transport your harvested vegetables to the market, what precautions would you take to keep them fresh?
2. How would you sort and grade your harvested vegetables to ensure that they are ready for consumption or sale?
3. What packaging materials would you use to protect your harvested vegetables from damage?
4. If you were to store your harvested vegetables for some days, what conditions would you use to keep them fresh?
5. How would you process your harvested vegetables to extend their shelf life?
6. How would you market your harvested vegetables to minimise losses?
7. If you were to use a new technology in your postharvest practices, how would you ensure that it does not damage your vegetables?
8. How would you adapt postharvesting practices if you were to establish a new vegetable garden?

Write a summary of what you have learnt from this chapter in your portfolio.
