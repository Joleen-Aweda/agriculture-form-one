3.

You are responsible for feeding a flock of 100 mature-layer chickens of medium-sized breed.

Calculate the daily feed requirements based on their age and propose a feeding schedule that ensures optimal nutrition and egg production.

4.

You are managing a layer farm and you need to optimise the space and feed for the chickens.

Consider the impact of space and feed on their behaviour and egg production.

Design a proper space and feed plan for the chickens

5.

You are planning to start a layer farm and you need to determine the appropriate stocking rate for light, medium and heavy breeds of layer chickens.

(a) Consider the space requirements, breed characteristics, and management implications.

(b) Propose a stocking rate plan for each type.

6.

You are inspecting a layer flock and you need to identify non-laying birds for removal:

(a) Develop a strategy to differentiate between laying and non-laying birds based on physical characteristics, behaviour, or other indicators.

(b) Explain the importance of removing non-laying birds from the flock.

7.

You are a farm owner and you need to plan for the replacement of a layer flock.

(a) Determine the age at which well-managed layers typically stop laying profitably and explain the reasons behind this decline.

(b) Justify the importance of replacing the flock at the appropriate time.

8.

You are responsible for handling and adding value to table eggs produced on a layer farm.

(a) Identify the key aspects of egg handling, packaging, and storage that ensure quality and safety.

(b) Develop a plan to add value to the eggs and explain the benefits of proper storage practices.

9.

Your neighbour (layer chicken farmer) has been running a chicken enterprise for a year and is facing a problem with selling eggs.

(a) Describe a creative solution to improve this process and ensure a profitable income from the eggs.

(b) Provide step-by-step pieces of advice.

10.

To keep the chickens in your school and home healthy, you need to make a plan to protect them from parasites and diseases.

Propose a complete plan which will enable you to achieve this purpose.
