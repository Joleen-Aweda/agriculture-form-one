You cut off the whole head at the soil level. This is best when the leaves are strong and before the seed stalks start to grow, usually 50 - 100 days after planting.

Postharvest management of Chinese cabbage includes the following:

  • (a)

    Cleaning: Clean the cabbage with clean and safe water before packaging. Always wash your hands before and after handling Chinese cabbages.

  • (b)

    Packaging: Place harvested cabbages in clean containers. Avoid overloading.

  • (c)

    Storing: Use cooling facilities which may include refrigerator, charcoal evaporative cooler or zero-energy cooling chamber for storage.

  • (d)

    Sun drying: Dry cabbages by slicing and spreading in the sun. Store dried cabbages in airtight containers or bags.

  • (e)

    Marketing: Continue to identify more potential buyers in local markets. Conduct market surveys. Keep in touch with buyers.
