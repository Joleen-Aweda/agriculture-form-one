Nest

a structure or place made or chosen by a bird for laying eggs and sheltering its youngs

Nursery

a piece of land prepared for raising seedlings which normally would not be sown/planted directly into the field

Nutrient

a substance that provides nourishment essential for the growth, maintenance, and functioning of organisms, including vitamins, minerals, proteins and energy

Off season vegetable production

production of vegetable crops before or after their normal season of cultivation

Organic eggs

eggs produced by chickens that are fed organic feeds in a free-range system

Organic matter

decomposed plant and animal materials in soil, rich in carbon compounds, supporting soil structure, fertility, and microbial life

Parasite

an organism that lives in or on another organism (host) and derives nutrients at the host’s expense that may cause harm

Pathogen

a microorganism, such as a virus, bacteria, fungus, or parasite, that causes disease in a host organism, including animals and plants

Perches

horizontal rods or branches installed in bird houses, allowing birds to rest, roost, or perch off the ground

Pest

a harmful organism, such as weeds, insects, rodents or birds, that damages crops, livestock, or property, requiring control or eradication measures

Pesticides

chemicals or substances used to control, repel, or kill pests such as insects, weeds, fungi, and rodents in agriculture

pH

a measure of the concentration of hydrogen ions (that is, protons) in a substance

Phenotype

observable characteristics that can easily be seen or measured
