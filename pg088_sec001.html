The seeds can be threshed and winnowed, then stored in a cool, dry place.

Value addition can be through processing amaranth into flour or using it to make various food products.

Under good care and the right conditions, one hectare of amaranth can produce around 25 tons of green leaves and 2 to 5 tons of grain (seeds).
