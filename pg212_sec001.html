The more you understand about parasite and disease management in indigenous chickens, the better you can care for these chickens. If you provide good housing and food for the chickens, they can be healthier. Most importantly, you can protect them from deadly diseases, for example, Newcastle and Fowl pox by giving them vaccines. These vaccines have been shown to work well against viral diseases. It is also advisable to deworm all the chickens. This can reduce problems caused by intestinal parasites and diseases. Also, it can improve the chickens’ performance. You can control external parasites such as mites and fleas with proper pesticides. To get rid of these parasites, you should also spray the chicken house, especially hidden spots. You can control diseases in other ways too. You could buy healthy flocks from clean farms, keep high hygiene standards, avoid using contaminated or mouldy feeds, dispose of dead birds properly, and treat sick birds early.

Breeding management in indigenous chickens

A good breeding plan can help to improve the meat and egg quality of indigenous chickens. Breeding is a complex process that requires specialised knowledge and skills. In this section, you will focus on the basic process of mating hens and cocks, and managing the incubation of eggs.

Mating of indigenous chickens: It is advisable to use the best cocks and hens for the production of eggs for the next group of chicks, based on what the farmer wants to achieve. The farmer should have at least one cock for every ten hens. In this way, most of the eggs will be fertilised and thus will be able to hatch into chicks.
