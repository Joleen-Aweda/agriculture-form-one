Activity 10.5

Prepare a plan for maintaining the health of chickens in a small-scale chicken farm.

This could be at your school or home.

Your plan should include steps to ensure the chickens have enough space, receive the right amount of feed, and are protected from external and internal parasites.

It should also include ways to keep the chicken house clean and fresh, and how to keep the chickens busy and happy.

Exercise 10.4

1.

You are a backyard chicken owner and you notice that one of your chickens is showing signs of illness. It is not eating, its feathers are ruffled, and it seems extremely tired. What could be the possible health issue, and what steps can you take to address it?

2.

You are a small-scale chicken farmer and you have recently introduced new chickens to your flock. However, you notice that the new chickens are being pecked and bullied by the existing ones. What could be the cause of this behaviour, and how can you manage it to ensure the well-being of all the chickens?

3.

You are a student studying poultry farming and you need to assess the health of a chicken for a class project. How would you conduct a basic health check on the chicken? What signs would you look for, and what steps would you take to ensure its well-being?

Write a summary of what you have learnt from this chapter in your portfolio.
