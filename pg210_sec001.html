Although most households keep indigenous chickens in free range, the most convenient system to raise indigenous chickens is a semi-intensive system. This means having a small house for them to sleep in at night, and a space for them to run around during the day. A square metre can accommodate 8 chickens. If the house is provided with perches, a square metre can accommodate more chickens. An ideal house for indigenous chickens should be constructed on well-drained soils if it is not to be lifted off the ground. The house should have open sides with solid walls that cover about one-third of the wall. It should have good ventilation but protect the chickens from strong winds. The roof should be strong to prevent rain from entering the house. Lastly, the house should be designed to keep vermin and predators out. It is advisable to use locally available construction materials. The house should be provided with properly managed laying nests and perches. Make sure there is some good grass in the runs for chickens to eat.
