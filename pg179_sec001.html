Thereafter, reduce the light by 2 hours every week until 12 hours of day length are reached on the 8th week. Light brightness should be high enough to enable chicks to easily find feed and water. After a few days, the brightness can be reduced. The light lighting schedule recommended during this period is shown in Table 11.1. Lighting requirements differ depending on breed, therefore, follow the lighting programme given by the chick distributor or poultry expert. Management of humidity for chicks

The best relative humidity for the chicks is between 70% and 80%. High humidity prevents dehydration and cannibalism. To maintain this high humidity in the first days, it may be necessary to place a pan filled with water near the heat source or spray water against the walls and or floors to increase humidity. As birds grow, the extra humidity brought up by the droppings and breathing out air will need to be removed by good ventilation. Wet litter may be the result of high humidity above the recommendation. This may result in high chances of diseases such as coccidiosis.

Management of feeding for layer chicks

The chicks should be fed chick starter mash from day one up to the 28th day (4 weeks). The chick mash contains high contents of protein, energy, minerals, vitamins and trace elements as additives.
